\section{Conclusion}

Scale correction in chart QA is directional and regime-dependent. In answer-tuned Qwen2.5-VL-7B, prefer-large scale-contrastive adaptation recovers most blind-verified strict test-set scale errors, and compute-matched controls attribute the effect to directional supervision rather than extra training. Globally, the same adapter's aggregate effect flips sign with a set's direction composition. Blind sampling shows the model usually already generates the correct scale; verifiers, calculators, and label-free selectors recover only a small, source-specific part of that headroom. For percentage--decimal, factor-of-100 errors on ChartQA-like data, reliable scale-regime selection is the central open problem. Systems should evaluate scale reliability by regime with fix--break accounting, not deploy automatic correction on aggregate gains.
